NAS Review — Beta v0.2.61

Use the sidebar + button to bookmark a folder, then start Review. K = Keep & Next; D = request permanent delete; 0 = Timeline Preview.

Review sources: Finder-mounted SMB shares. Review supports a persistent skip-confirmation setting for D in the sidebar, disabled by default. Review decisions and JPEG thumbnail cache are stored on your Mac.

Counts and completion: Review shows the remaining non-deleted/non-missing video count while retaining stable snapshot positions. Natural EOF or a deliberate seek to the final seconds of a verified loaded pending queue video records KEEP. Seeking to the middle, unrelated media and duplicate EOF do not.

Warning: D executes /bin/rm after native confirmation by default, or without the confirmation if explicitly enabled. It permanently deletes a video; it does not use Trash and is not atomic identity-conditional deletion. Use only disposable files and keep NAS backups.

Directory mode: In the sidebar, click a folder to select, double-click to enter, and press D or Delete selected folder. This recursively removes all contents via /bin/rm -r only after two separate native confirmations; the video skip-confirmation preference never applies. Unfinished Review checkpoints must be resolved first. Directory deletion is not atomic and cannot be undone.

Timeline previews are generated in the background on video load. FFmpeg is expected at /opt/homebrew/bin/ffmpeg.
