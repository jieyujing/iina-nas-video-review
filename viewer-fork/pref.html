NAS Review — Beta v0.2.58

Use the sidebar + button to bookmark a folder, then start Review. K = Keep & Next; D = request permanent delete; 0 = Timeline Preview.

Review sources: Finder-mounted SMB shares. Both recursive and nonrecursive Review support D for the playing video; Review decisions and JPEG thumbnail cache are stored on your Mac.

Warning: D executes /bin/rm after a native confirmation. It permanently deletes a video; it does not use Trash and is not atomic identity-conditional deletion. Use only disposable files and keep NAS backups.

Timeline previews are generated in the background on video load. FFmpeg is expected at /opt/homebrew/bin/ffmpeg.
