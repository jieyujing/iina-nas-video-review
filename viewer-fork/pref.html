NAS Review — Beta v0.2.67

Use the sidebar + button to bookmark a folder, then start Review. K = Keep & Next; D = request permanent delete; 0 = Timeline Preview.

Review sources: Finder-mounted SMB shares. Review supports a persistent skip-confirmation setting for D in the sidebar, disabled by default. Review decisions and JPEG thumbnail cache are stored on your Mac.

Counts and completion: Review shows the remaining non-deleted/non-missing video count while retaining stable snapshot positions. Natural EOF or seeking a verified loaded pending video to the final seconds (including exact 100%) records KEEP, even when mpv omits the final time-pos callback. Stop, mid-video seeking, unrelated media and stale EOF do not.

Serialized background delete queue: D queues the currently loaded SMB video and advances immediately; K and further D remain available during other deletions. One worker performs mount, target identity, and file checks before each permanent /bin/rm. Tasks are saved locally and displayed as QUEUED, RUNNING, DELETED, FAILED or UNKNOWN. UNKNOWN blocks additional deletes without automatic retries; interrupted tasks require verified manual recovery after restart.

Restart recovery (v0.2.65): Review resumes at a non-reserved pending item without rewinding finished progress. UNKNOWN reconciliation targets the durable background job, verifies the same SMB share and original pathname, and requests manual confirmation. Queued but not issued tasks remain paused; no deletion is automatically retried.

Delete queue completion (v0.2.66): A persistent Review banner distinguishes IDLE, WORKING, PAUSED, BLOCKED, COMPLETE and COMPLETE_WITH_WARNINGS, with queue counts. Only fully settled durable deletion results show Safe to close IINA. FAILED or manually reconciled jobs never claim successful deletion; paused and UNKNOWN work require operator review, not waiting.

Compact Review layout (v0.2.67): The main sidebar shows remaining/KEEP/deleted counters, K and D actions, a concise deletion status, and the scrollable video list. NAS backup controls, shortcut diagnostics and extended deletion guidance live in the collapsed Advanced settings & Help section below the list. All destructive safeguards remain active.

Warning: D executes /bin/rm after native confirmation by default, or without the confirmation if explicitly enabled. It permanently deletes a video; it does not use Trash and is not atomic identity-conditional deletion. Use only disposable files and keep NAS backups.

Directory mode: In the sidebar, click a folder to select, double-click to enter, and press D or Delete selected folder. This recursively removes all contents via /bin/rm -r only after two separate native confirmations; the video skip-confirmation preference never applies. Unfinished Review checkpoints must be resolved first. Directory deletion is not atomic and cannot be undone.

Timeline previews are generated in the background on video load. FFmpeg is expected at /opt/homebrew/bin/ffmpeg.
